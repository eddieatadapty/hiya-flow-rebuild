Your Apple Ads, in one paragraph. In the US Apple Ads data we see for Hiya Inc between 10 October and 10 November 2026, you show on about 13% of searches for your own name, “hiya: spam blocker & caller id”, so most people looking for you meet someone else’s ad first. On “truecaller”, the most popular term on your list, you show on under 1%. Some terms bring people who aren’t looking for a call blocker at all: “call the shots”, “scream”, “norcal”, plus Arabic and Chinese searches. That data is account-level, so it can’t tell Hiya from Mr. Number. The search layer carries no range: only a test per search can give one.
